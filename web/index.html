Chapter one · how it measures

From a shaky clip to cubic metres per second.

Four steps, each with its evidence. Every figure in this chapter comes from one development run on video 8 of the Video Globe Challenge, a Norwegian brook: 300 frames, ten seconds.

i.

Hold the banks still.

ALIKED keypoints are matched with LightGlue on the banks only, never on the water, and each keyframe is registered to the reference view, so camera shake can't become velocity. On this clip the frozen build rejected the feature model and treated the camera as static. It was hand-held and drifts. That miss is the clearest open issue in our report.

ii.

Flatten the surface at today's water level.

Surveyed ground reference points fix the camera model. Each pixel is projected onto the water plane at the measured stage, so a distance on the image becomes a distance on the river. When the river rises, the plane rises with it.

iii.

Measure it three ways that fail differently.

Window correlation, streak angles in space-time images, and dense optical flow. A visible riverbed fools correlation but not streaks; glare fools both in different ways. The agent compares them before it trusts any of them.

iv.

Integrate across the surveyed section.

Surface velocity, times a coefficient for the slower water below, across the surveyed bed at the measured level. The uncertainty is carried with the number, never dropped.

Chapter two · the agent refuses

It would rather say nothing than say it wrong.

The agent picks methods and parameters, but it can't publish on its own say-so. Every action passes a policy gate written in code. A gauging goes out only when the evidence in the run supports the basis the agent claims.

Publish
methods_agree
LSPIV 0.724 · STIV 0.828 m³/s · spread 13.5 % of a 15 % tolerance

Two independent methods agree, so the mean of the agreeing pair is published with its uncertainty.

v.

A warning needs a signature.

After each published gauging the station refits its rating curve and runs a stage nowcast. If the chance of crossing the flood threshold within three hours is at least one half, it drafts a warning and the workflow stops on a task token. Only a reviewer with the passcode can issue it; the agent has no tool for it. Unsigned drafts expire after six hours.
