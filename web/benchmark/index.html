Protocol

  1. Videos 1 and 8 were used for development.
  2. Station configs for videos 2 to 7 were built from survey inputs only (GRPs, bathymetry, water level and site notes), without opening any results file.
  3. The agent, the policy and the parameters were frozen: git tag eval-freeze.
  4. Videos 2 to 7 were scored once per brain. Only eval/ reads the answer key.
  5. Every run, of any version, is appended to eval/runs.jsonl.
  6. Runs tagged v1.1 came after fixes prompted by the refusal suite. They are not blind, and are reported next to the frozen runs, never in place of them.
